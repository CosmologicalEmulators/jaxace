    Scalar growth sensitivity convergence¶

    Original D/Dprime NumPy/SciPy DOP853 oracle, restarted at every Akima knot; complex-step mass derivatives.

    Environment: jax 0.6.2, jaxlib 0.6.2, diffrax 0.7.0, quadax 0.2.13, scipy 1.18.0. CPU float64.

    Both schemes use the frozen scalar thermal prescription; the public JAX polynomial table is rebuilt at import.
